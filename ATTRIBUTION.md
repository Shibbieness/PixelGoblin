# Attribution

Short version: **keep the credit line where a user can find it.**

```
Built on PixelGoblin — © Shibbieness / M MAOU LLC
```

## Free (AGPL-3.0) use

This is an **additional term under AGPL-3.0 §7(b)**, which permits a license
to require "preservation of specified reasonable legal notices or author
attributions."

Preserve the credit line in at least one place a user would reasonably look:

- an About box, credits screen, or `--version` / `--about` output;
- an `/about` or `/credits` page for a network service;
- the README, docs, or a `NOTICE` / `THIRD-PARTY` file shipped with the work;
- source file headers, if you redistribute source.

That is the whole obligation. No splash screen, no watermark on sprites, no
marketing mention. Sprites you make carry no credit requirement from the
engine at all.

## Commercially licensed use

A commercial license waives the share-alike condition; in exchange the credit
gets more visible for large products (placeholder: over USD $1,000,000/year
gross) — product credits, a "powered by" line, the store listing, or the docs
landing page. Exact terms live in the commercial agreement.

## Why it is split this way

AGPL §7 allows only a short list of added terms. Attribution preservation is
on it; a revenue-triggered marketing credit is not, and bolting one onto the
free license would make it "badgeware" and push it out of GPL compatibility.
Keeping the larger obligation in the private commercial contract avoids that.
This is the same split QRen Coder and Vanilla Core use.
